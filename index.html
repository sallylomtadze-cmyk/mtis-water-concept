    About MTIS

    A hidden treasure
    beneath Natakhtari

    Deep beneath the rolling hills of Georgia's Natakhtari region lies a hidden treasure, MTIS Natural Mineral Water. Sourced from ancient subterranean rock formations, this extraordinary water is the result of centuries of geological evolution. Filtered naturally through mineral rich layers shaped by volcanic activity and tectonic shifts, every drop of MTIS is purified by nature long before it reaches the surface.

    As rainwater seeps through the earth, it embarks on a slow, transformative journey through the region's complex geological strata. Along the way, it absorbs essential minerals that not only enrich its taste but also contribute to its health benefits. The result is a perfectly balanced, naturally mineralized water, untouched by human hands, preserved in its purest form.

    Lowest bromate in Georgia

    Bromate is a chemical compound that contains the bromate ion. It can form in water during disinfection, when ozone or chlorine reacts with bromide that is naturally present in some water sources. Bromate is considered a potential human carcinogen and studies have linked it to cancer in laboratory animals. MTIS is proud to be the Georgian brand with the least amount of bromate in it, close to 0%.
